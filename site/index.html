Readings every 5 minutes in Pacific Standard Time, refreshed automatically while the app is open. Gauges report with up to a 2 hour delay. Lake and reservoir gauges measure water level only, and reservoir levels are controlled by BC Hydro. Marks and bookmarks are saved on this device only. The Englishman River bench landmark (about 2.45 m, 134 m³/s) is estimated from the December 2025 high water, when the river came up to just below the bench. Marks are a guide, not official flood levels. Rain: Environment Canada GEM model via Open-Meteo. Tides: Fisheries and Oceans Canada predictions. Map: © OpenStreetMap contributors, © CARTO. Advisories, return periods and 5-day forecasts: BC River Forecast Centre. Typical and record highs: Environment Canada yearly peak records (typical = median of yearly peaks).
